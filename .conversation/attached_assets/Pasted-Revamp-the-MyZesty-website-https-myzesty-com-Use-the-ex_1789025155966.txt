Revamp the MyZesty website: https://myzesty.com/

Use the existing MyZesty website as the source for product content, creative assets, examples, and inspiration. You can improve the structure and add relevant sections where needed.

## Main Design Direction

Follow the visual and layout approach shown in the attached Higgsfield reference.

The website should feel:

* Dark and premium
* Highly visual and creative
* Image and video-led
* Editorial and curated
* Dense with creative content
* Modern and cinematic
* Minimal in copy
* Focused on showcasing creative possibilities

Use **Higgsfield as the main visual inspiration**, especially its:

* Dark interface
* Large visual grids
* Masonry-style content layouts
* Mixed image and video galleries
* Creative showcases
* Editorial section structure
* Minimal typography
* Small, focused labels and CTAs
* Content-first storytelling

## Important Design Rules

* Do **not** overload the page with AI buzzwords or unnecessary jargon.
* Avoid phrases like "next-generation AI," "revolutionary AI," "AI-powered future," or similar generic marketing language.
* Do **not** use excessive AI gradients, glowing backgrounds, neon effects, or generic futuristic visuals.
* Avoid making every section look like a colorful SaaS card.
* Do **not** rely heavily on product UI screenshots or dashboard screens.

Instead, follow the attached reference pattern by using:

**Creative outputs, generated images, videos, campaigns, stories, ads, characters, products, and visual examples as the main content of the page.**

The visuals should communicate what MyZesty can create.

## Content Approach

Build the page around visual creative categories and outcomes from MyZesty, such as:

* AI Images
* AI Videos
* Ads and Marketing Campaigns
* Product Promotion
* Storytelling and Story+
* Creative Editing
* Templates and Inspiration
* Different creative styles and visual outcomes

Each section should primarily showcase a curated collection of visuals rather than long explanations.

Use short headings, minimal descriptions, and clear CTAs.

## Visual Layout

Create a scrolling experience similar to the attached reference:

* Strong hero section
* Large visual showcase sections
* Masonry and grid-based creative galleries
* Different image sizes and aspect ratios
* Full-width visual moments
* Featured campaigns or collections
* Curated creative categories
* Occasional promotional or conversion sections
* Minimal footer with feature exploration

The page should feel like users are **exploring what is possible with MyZesty**, rather than reading a traditional feature list.

## Goal

Create a visually rich landing page that positions MyZesty as a serious creative platform.

Prioritize:

**Creative work → Inspiration → Possibilities → Exploration → Action**

The final design should take strong inspiration from the attached Higgsfield layout and content pattern while maintaining a unique MyZesty identity, branding, and product positioning.

Focus on showing what users can create, not explaining AI.
